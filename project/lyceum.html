The Royal Lyceum Theatre

Heritage Trail



The brief for this project was to create a six stop heritage trail around the public areas of The Royal Lyceum Theatre, aimed towards families, tourists and theatre enthusiasts, to highlight the history of the theatre. As The Royal Lyceum Theatre is one of Edinburgh’s longest standing theatres, I wanted to use this long running legacy to inform my approach for this project. The trail focuses on the work that happens behind the scenes of a theatre show, and the evolution it has seen over the years.
